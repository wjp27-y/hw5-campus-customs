I predicted the agents that the Boss would assign to each Ticket, but they ended up using more tools than anticipated. I think Ticket 102 would've been simpler as one agent since that was just checking rent and adjusting the cash balance, which is what we did in an earlier assignment with one agent instead of 3.

I think 3 problems the Campus Customs store could have that the agent team would solve would be: paying employee salaries/adjusting cash balance accordingly (Accounting agent), paying electricity (Accounting and Facilities agents) and organizing a flash sale (Accounting, Customer Service, and Inventory agents). Since agents can understand how cash balance changes, they can understand how it would change in different contexts—paying electricity is just like paying rent, as are employee salaries. As proven by Ticket 103, the agent team is capable of projecting the impact of discounts and fluctuations in supply and sales.

Three new problems that the agents could not solve on their own are screening job applications, which would require a resume tool, employee info fetch tool, and a dedicated staff manager agent. These agents and tools would also be needed to fire employees. Finally, if the store were under threat of robbery, an agent would be needed to manage security systems, with corresponding tools to fetch and analyze security camera feed and alarm activity.
